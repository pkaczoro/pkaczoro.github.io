Privacy policy

MailSender is a private tool used only by its owner. It uses the Gmail API scope gmail.send solely to send messages approved by its operator from one dedicated account.

It does not read, store or share mailbox contents. Sending records (recipient address, subject, status) are kept in the operator's own database for delivery tracking and are not shared with third parties.
